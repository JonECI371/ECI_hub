Before you book

Your employer must register you on SkillGuard for Utilities and pay the subscription first. Bring your SkillGuard number and photo ID on the day. Without both, the passport cannot be issued.

Who needs this

Anyone with site access

Every direct employee, contractor and supply chain partner must hold a valid passport before starting work on the Thames Water network. If your work involves site access, you need it.

Course detail

What you will cover

01

Site hazards

Hazards specific to the Thames Water network and the controls that manage them.

02

Safety expectations

Zero incidents, zero harm, zero compromise, and what that means in practice.

03

Environment

Environmental responsibilities and protecting the public water supply.

04

Behaviours

Behavioural and customer facing standards expected of everyone on site.
