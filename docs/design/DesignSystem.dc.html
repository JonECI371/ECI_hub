Brand motif

A faint zone hatch on dark bands. Used on heroes only, never behind body text.

Rules that keep it consistent

  • Cards float on the page colour with a soft shadow. Borders are for fields only.
  • Dark bands only on marketing and celebration pages. Forms get a light header so the form is the focus.
  • One delivery-type chip style everywhere: cards, calendar, dashboards, admin.
  • Cyan is for actions and focus. Text on cyan is always ink.
  • Every interactive element shows a 3px cyan focus ring.
